# 🧑‍🏫 ClassBridge AI

## Real-Time Collaborative Learning Platform for Coding & Technology Education

**ClassBridge AI** is a collaborative education platform developed by **SMAPIS Technologies — Smart Multi-Agent Platform & Intelligent Systems**.

The platform is designed to improve live technology education by allowing teachers to observe, guide, review and support students while they work on coding assignments, software projects and digital-learning activities in real time.

ClassBridge AI combines live instruction, collaborative coding, student monitoring, assignments, progress tracking and AI-assisted learning within one learning environment.

Its core idea is simple:

**Teach → Observe → Guide → Correct → Assess → Improve**

---

# 🎯 The Problem

Remote coding classes often rely on tools such as video conferencing and screen sharing.

While these tools are useful, they create several limitations:

* Teachers may struggle to see what every student is doing
* Students often need to share screens individually
* Instructors cannot easily monitor multiple learners at once
* Coding mistakes may go unnoticed for long periods
* Student progress can be difficult to measure
* Assignments and live-class work are often disconnected
* Parents may have limited visibility into learner progress

ClassBridge AI is designed to provide a more structured environment for practical technology education.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

ClassBridge AI creates a collaborative digital classroom where teachers and students can work together during practical coding sessions.

Teachers can supervise learner activity while students complete coding exercises, projects and assignments.

The platform is designed to complement live communication tools such as Google Meet while providing deeper visibility into the student’s actual work.
